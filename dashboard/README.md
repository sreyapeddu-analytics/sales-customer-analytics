# Dashboard

This folder documents the KPI dashboard for the project. The overview figure
(`../images/kpi_dashboard.png`) mimics the top of a BI dashboard: headline
KPI tiles plus the monthly revenue trend. The remaining charts in `../images/`
are the drill-down visuals behind each tile.

## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition | Value (2023–2024) |
|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | `SUM(line_total)` over all orders | $2,322,744.42 |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | `AVG(line_total)` per order | $193.56 |
| Total Orders | `COUNT(order_id)` | 12,000 |
| Repeat-buyer Rate | Share of purchasing customers with > 1 order | 83.9% (1,597 of 1,903) |
| Repeat-buyer Revenue Share | Share of revenue from customers with > 1 order | 97.3% |
| YoY Monthly Growth | `(revenue_2024 − revenue_2023) / revenue_2023` per calendar month | Dec: +18.4%, Nov: +4.1% |

### RFM segments

Customers are scored 1–5 on **Recency** (days since last order, as of
2024-12-31), **Frequency** (order count), and **Monetary** (total spend) using
quintiles, then grouped:

| Segment | RFM score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Champions | 13–15 | Bought recently, often, and a lot — protect them |
| Loyal Customers | 11–12 | Buy regularly — grow with upsell |
| Potential Loyalists | 9–10 | Recent buyers to nurture into loyalists |
| At Risk | 7–8 | Used to buy, gone quiet — win-back candidates (289 customers) |
| Lost / Inactive | 3–6 | Dormant — reactivation or suppression |
